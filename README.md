A program (Node 22, no dependencies) that writes one line to
`/output/entry.txt` — the single line its writer would send back to their
younger self if one line were all they got.

## The line

> Your father was wrong about the guitar, right about the savings account — keep both lessons, drop the grudge.

109 characters, 111 bytes, no trailing newline.

## Why this line wins against the judge brief

The judge weighs, most important first: a line actually told to a younger
self, something only this older self could say, one breath, a turn that
stays, and honesty that stings a little.

- **Only this older self could say it.** A father wrong about the guitar
  and right about the savings account is a contradiction from one lived
  life — not a motto anyone could wear.
- **Turn and release.** Wrong/right, then "keep both lessons, drop the
  grudge": the verdict and the letting-go land in the same breath.
- **Stings without sentimentality.** The grudge is the younger self's, and
  the older self is telling him to put it down.
- **Stands alone.** One sentence, no context, no explanation, no wasted word.
